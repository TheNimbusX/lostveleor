## Final prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: one finished YouTube thumbnail, landscape 16:9, ideally 1920x1080. This is for the FIRST presentation video of an indie action roguelike in development called The War Remains. Main visual hook: a very simple early blockout becoming a beautiful stylized fantasy game. Create a striking, professional thumbnail that reads instantly at 160px wide, using the actual supplied game references, not a generic fantasy painting.
Input image 1 (amber camp): primary visual and identity reference. Preserve its distinctive isometric hand-painted 3D camp: burgundy and cream merchant tent with lanterns and barrels, stone-ring campfire, stone paths, lush fir foliage and golden evening lighting. Use a closer, clear crop/recomposition of THESE existing elements as the large dominant right 65-70% of the thumbnail. Bright amber light against rich deep forest greens and blue shadows. Keep the game-like isometric camera and its actual painterly style, not photorealistic, not anime. The merchant tent and campfire should be big and readable, not tiny details.
Input image 2 (early Unity prototype): faithful supporting insert of the real first-days prototype. In the lower-left 30-35%, show a large slightly tilted inset/cutout of this blockout: two primitive stalls, red box buildings, green circular pad, beige path and little campfire. Keep its deliberately crude forms and flat colors, muted cool gray background. Crop away editor toolbars, icons, cursor and window chrome. Do not improve or beautify the blockout. It is the honest early version.
Layout: dark deep blue/charcoal negative space across upper-left around 42% width, seamlessly transitioning diagonally into the saturated warm camp on the right. At upper-left, huge heavyweight condensed Cyrillic sans-serif headline in exactly two lines: "ИГРА" (warm white) then "С НУЛЯ" (bold golden yellow). Verbatim spelling, crisp professionally typeset, no warped characters. Headline must occupy about 23-27% of total image area, comfortable 5% margins, readable on mobile; minimal dark shadow for separation, no ornate fantasy lettering. The prototype inset sits clearly below the text. Add one simple confident thick white curved arrow near the lower middle, directing the eye from the primitive prototype toward the beautiful camp on the right; not covering the headline or the merchant tent. Make the diagonal change in visual quality evident without extra labels.
Constraints: one cohesive final thumbnail, not a moodboard or multiple alternatives. Exactly the three words ИГРА С НУЛЯ and no other text. No invented dates, completion claims, earnings, time limits or AI badges. No extra logos, no faces or reaction person, no new monsters or scenery, no fake battle, no HUD or browser/editor UI, no visual clutter, no watermarks. Keep the bottom-right 13% x 12% free of essential text/details for YouTube's duration badge. Punchy but tasteful contrast and saturation. The wow comes from the game's warm attractive environment and the honest visual transformation, not from explosions or generic glowing outlines.
