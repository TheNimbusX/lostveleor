## Notes
- Weakest slots: **hero stunned** (Epidemic has no organic "daze ring"; the options are synthetic tinnitus or metal bells), **thorn spikes erupting** (no dedicated "spikes from ground" file; build it from twig-snap runs plus the spade-into-dirt hits), **Stonehoof death** (the pig scream may read too real or sad; the wood layer carries it) and **Forest Bud death #3** (tagged Gore even though it is a vegetable recording).
- Avoid: most of the "Claw Attack" series (Gore 01 to 03, Skin, Rip Fabric), "Bug squish" results (almost all Gore) and the "Cartoon Pop" family, which is too silly.
- Other finds worth knowing: `Footsteps, Animal, Foley, Crustaceans, Grass, Singular Footsteps` (22.7 s, query "moss"), which could be steps for the Root Snarer stump-crab, and `Magic, Shimmer, Spell, Pad, Healing Magic, Idle, Complex 01` (21.0 s, query "nature magic"), an alternative heal loop.
