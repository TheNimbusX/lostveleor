`whirlwind_pelag_pcm.wav` is a runtime PCM conversion of the project-provided
curated source from
`ART/designed-images/assets/art-brief-v05/PELAG/sfx/whirlewind.mp3`. It is not
covered by the CC0 statement above; its usage follows the source pack's project
license.

The source archives used for this project are retained under
`artifacts/audio-source/` for provenance and future remixing.

## Epidemic Sound (owner's subscription, 24 September 2026)

`Pelag/Whirlwind_01..02.wav`, `Pelag/WhirlwindPulse_01..03.wav` and
`Pelag/WhirlwindHit_01..03.wav` are layered from the owner's own sabre
recordings (`Pelag/Attack_01..05.wav`), the CC0 `HitBody`/`HitMetal` clips above
and two Epidemic Sound recordings chosen by the owner («Designed, Impact, Hit,
Low, Deep Thuds, Boom» as a low-end layer, «Swooshes, Swish, Airy, Short, Mid
Freq 01» as a quiet air layer). The Epidemic material is licensed to this
project through the owner's Epidemic Sound subscription, not CC0. The untouched
sources (including three recordings that are no longer used) are kept in
`ART/PELAG/sfx/2026-09-24-whirlwind/`; the build script is
`tools/audio/build-whirlwind-sfx.py`.
